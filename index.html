Reading comprehension · Early readers

Automatic scoring of children's spoken answers to story questions

A child reads a short story and answers a question out loud. An LLM judge reads the story, the question and the transcribed answer, and returns a score of 0, 1 or 2, the same scale trained annotators use.

Agreement with human annotators

How it works

Three inputs in, one score out

The judge sees the same information a teacher would: the passage, the question that was asked, and what the child said.

The scoring scale

What 2, 1 and 0 mean

Real answers from the evaluation set, with the score both human annotators gave.

Question types

Literal and inferential questions

LiteralThe answer is stated in the story

The child has to recall a detail from the text.

What did Stan lose?

InferentialThe answer needs reasoning

The child has to connect ideas or explain why. These are harder to score.

Why do fish hide in the mud or bushes?

Integration

What FableVision sends and gets back

FableVision sends the inputs and reads back the score. No judge setup is needed on their side.
